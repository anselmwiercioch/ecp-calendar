How to subscribe in Google Calendar

  1. Copy the Subscribe URL above
  2. Open Google Calendar on desktop
  3. Click the + next to "Other calendars" in the left sidebar
  4. Choose "From URL"
  5. Paste the Subscribe URL and click Add calendar

Note: Google Calendar checks subscribed calendars for updates roughly every 12–24 hours.
